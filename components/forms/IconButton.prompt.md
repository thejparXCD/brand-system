**IconButton** — a single-icon button for toolbars, cards, and nav. Always provide `aria-label`.

```jsx
<IconButton aria-label="Search" variant="ghost"><Icon name="search" /></IconButton>
<IconButton aria-label="Next" variant="solid" round><Icon name="arrow-right" /></IconButton>
```

Variants: `solid` · `outline` · `ghost`. Sizes: `sm` (32) · `md` (40) · `lg` (48). `round` for circular.
