**PillarCard** — the brand's signature dark card for the three M's (Message / Machines / Mission) and the four service lines. Each `theme` carries its service-line gradient.

```jsx
<PillarCard
  theme="mission" index="M3" eyebrow="Why you exist"
  icon={<Icon name="compass" />}
  title="Mission"
  description="Align why you exist — purpose that drives momentum." />
```

Themes: `technology` · `growth` · `mission` · `data` · `momentum`. Always renders on the ink ground; pair three across in a grid.
